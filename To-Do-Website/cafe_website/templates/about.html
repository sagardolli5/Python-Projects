About Cafe Lists

Cafe Lists is a community-powered directory for discovering and sharing the most memorable cafes in your city. Whether you're a digital nomad, a coffee connoisseur, or just looking for a cosy corner — we have something for every kind of coffee lover.

Every listing is community-submitted and includes the details that matter most: where it is, when it's open, and what makes it worth the visit.
